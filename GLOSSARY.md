# Glossary

## Gold

The player's money. Rolled once at character creation, after Health Points, as d20 × 10
(10–200 Gold), and shown in the character stats summary. Spent at the Merchant.

## Maximum Health Points

The highest Health Points the player can have. Set to the Health Points rolled at character
creation. Healing never raises Health Points above it.

## Healing Potion

A consumable that restores d8 + 2 Health Points, never above Maximum Health Points. The
player carries a count of them, starting at 0, shown in the character and combat stats.
Bought from the Merchant for 30 Gold, or found on arriving at the south or north path (d20, 16 or
higher; each path gives at most one per game). Drunk during combat, using the player's
turn, or after a win or a retreat, before the game ends. It can't revive a defeated player.

## Merchant

The encounter on the south path. The Merchant offers Magical Armor, a Magical Weapon and a
Healing Potion, shows their prices and the player's Gold, and lets the player buy one item
or leave. After the visit the player returns to the path choice. Choices are numbered:
`1` armor, `2` weapon, `3` Healing Potion, `4` leave.

## Magical Armor

An item sold by the Merchant for 80 Gold. It reduces the damage of each dragon hit by 3,
never below 0. It doesn't change the Agility block roll.

## Magical Weapon

An upgrade sold by the Merchant for 120 Gold. It adds +4 to the Maximum Damage of the
player's current weapon, which keeps its occupation-specific type and ASCII art and is
shown as magical. It is not a replacement weapon.
