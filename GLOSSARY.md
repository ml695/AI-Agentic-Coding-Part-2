# Glossary

## Gold

The player's money. Rolled once at character creation, after Health Points, as d20 × 10
(10–200 Gold), and shown in the character stats summary. Spent at the Merchant.

## Merchant

The encounter on the south path. The Merchant offers Magical Armor and a Magical Weapon,
shows their prices and the player's Gold, and lets the player buy one item or leave.
After the visit the player returns to the path choice. Choices are numbered: `1` armor,
`2` weapon, `3` leave.

## Magical Armor

An item sold by the Merchant for 80 Gold. It reduces the damage of each dragon hit by 3,
never below 0. It doesn't change the Agility block roll.

## Magical Weapon

An upgrade sold by the Merchant for 120 Gold. It adds +4 to the Maximum Damage of the
player's current weapon, which keeps its occupation-specific type and ASCII art and is
shown as magical. It is not a replacement weapon.
