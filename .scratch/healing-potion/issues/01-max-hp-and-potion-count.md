# 01: Maximum Health Points and the potion count

**What to build:** The player gains **Maximum Health Points**, set to the Health Points rolled at character creation, and a **Healing Potion** count starting at 0. The character stats show Maximum HP alongside HP, and the potion count. The combat stats shown before the fight show the potion count. There's no way to get a potion yet. That comes in tickets 02 and 04. All text in four languages (spec Q2, Q6, Q7; user stories 1, 2).
